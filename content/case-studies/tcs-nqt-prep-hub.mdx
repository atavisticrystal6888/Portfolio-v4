## Who it's for, and why

TCS NQT Prep Hub is an open-source side project. I created and built it: the content system, the PWA and the progress analytics. Four contributors joined within the first week.

Every year, lakhs of engineering graduates sit the TCS National Qualifier Test, the gateway to Ninja (₹3.36 LPA), Digital (₹7–9 LPA) and Prime (₹9–11+ LPA) roles. Their job is to pass an exam with a known pattern, on a student budget, often on a phone.

The options fail them: paid coaching behind paywalls, scattered PDFs on Telegram, ad-heavy sites with dubious question quality.

The gap: no single free, offline-capable platform combined verified previous-year questions, structured study material and interactive practice, without sign-ups, ads or paywalls.

## What I learned before building

I surveyed 15+ TCS NQT aspirants about their pain points, audited PrepInsta, GeeksforGeeks and IndiaBix on content quality, UX and offline access, and mapped the exam: 92 questions across 6 sections (Numerical, Verbal, Reasoning, Programming Logic, Coding) in 150 minutes.

Two findings changed the plan. The top complaints were scattered resources, unverified questions and no way to track progress, so verification and progress had to be visible features. Most aspirants study on mobile, often on unreliable internet, so offline-first was non-negotiable.

## The bets, and what we cut

**Free, with no sign-up.** No paywall, no ads, no account; progress lives on the device.

**Offline-first.** For trains, hostels and patchy connectivity.

**Contributable by freshers.** Study content is plain Markdown, and the app has no framework to learn. Adding a question is a data-only change.

## What we built

A first-time user on a phone lands on the dashboard: progress cards, recent activity and topic-wise mastery. The quiz builder lets them pick topics, count, difficulty and time limit, or take a preset that reproduces the full exam: 92 questions in 150 minutes.

The question bank behind it: 74 verified PYQs from 2024 exam shifts with detailed explanations, 300 aptitude and reasoning questions, 50 coding problems, 10 full previous papers, HR interview prep and a "Know TCS" section.

The rest of the 12 features: flashcards, categorised notes, a Pomodoro timer, bookmarks, dark mode and analytics with topic mastery levels.

## How we knew it worked

The evidence is what a free, no-account product can produce. The app loads in under 1 second on 3G, installs as a PWA and works fully offline. Four contributors arrived within the first week and added PYQs, improved solutions and enhanced the app, the clearest sign that Markdown content lowered the barrier as intended.

The honest limits: no sign-ups and no backend means no count of users or quizzes taken; whether scores moved is not measured.

<UnderTheHood title="Architecture and implementation">

**1. Zero dependencies, vanilla JS only.** No React, no build tools, no bundlers. The entire app is `index.html` + `app.js` + `styles.css` + data files.

**2. Data-driven quiz engine.** All questions live in a structured JS data file. The quiz builder filters by topic, difficulty and count at runtime, so adding questions is a data-only change.

```javascript
// Quiz builder - supports custom configurations
const quizConfig = {
  topics: ['quantitative', 'reasoning', 'verbal', 'programming'],
  questionCount: 25,     // 10 / 25 / 50 / 100
  difficulty: 'mixed',   // easy / medium / hard / mixed
  timeLimit: 30,         // minutes
  mode: 'practice'       // practice (show answers) / test (timed)
};

// Quick-start presets for common patterns
const PRESETS = {
  'Full Mock':    { topics: 'all', count: 92, time: 150 },
  'Quick Quant':  { topics: ['quantitative'], count: 25, time: 30 },
  'Reasoning':    { topics: ['reasoning'], count: 30, time: 40 },
  'Programming':  { topics: ['programming'], count: 10, time: 20 },
};
```

**3. localStorage for persistence, no backend.** Quiz scores, flashcard progress, study time, bookmarks and notes persist in localStorage. Users can export and import their progress as JSON for backup or device migration.

**Offline.** The service worker caches the entire app shell and question bank on first load; once installed, the app works fully offline.

</UnderTheHood>
