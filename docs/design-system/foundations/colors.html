Light is the default identity ("working paper"); dark is the "night blueprint". The OS preference is ignored: the site ships data-theme="light" and only a stored choice in localStorage["ds-portfolio-theme"] switches it. Palette overrides (ocean, emerald, amber, mono) live in tokens.css but are not part of the shipped identity.
