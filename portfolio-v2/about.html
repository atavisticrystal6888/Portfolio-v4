How I Think About Product

Product Philosophy

Outcome > Output

Shipping features isn't the goal — shipping outcomes is. Every sprint, every decision should trace back to a measurable user or business impact.

Data Informs, Intuition Decides

Data removes noise, but it doesn't replace judgment. I use analytics to narrow the options, then conviction to pick the path that dashboards can't show.

Ship, Measure, Iterate

Perfect is the enemy of shipped. I bias toward getting something in front of users fast, measuring what happens, and iterating from real feedback.

Background

Who I Am

I am a final-year B.Tech student in Electronics & Computer Engineering at J.C. Bose University (YMCA), and I have spent the last two years embedding myself across product, analytics, and business functions.

At Wipro, I work as a Product Intern on the Aviation OS team — defining requirements, mapping user pain points, and streamlining discovery for platform masters. At Odena, I drive product execution, analytics, and community health metrics for a developer-focused product as an ongoing consultant.

Before that, I led equity and company analysis at Read Riches (growing content reach 4×), and enhanced lead generation 10× at Omniful.ai using data mining and Excel. Every role taught me the same lesson: impact comes from structured thinking, not from waiting for permission.

I pick up context fast, operate with low dependency, and bias toward shipping.
